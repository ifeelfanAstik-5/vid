Once upon a time, at the edge of the Whispering Woods, lived a tiny firefly named Pip. While all the other fireflies flashed in bright neon yellow, Pip glowed with a gentle, starlit lavender. 

The ancient forest had fallen into a deep, unending slumber under a heavy grey mist. The great Elder Oak had closed its leaves and sighed, "Unless the light of dawn touches each crown, the forest will sleep forever."

Pip carried a pocketful of moon-dew. Flying high into the branches, Pip fluttered to the highest bough of the Elder Oak and whispered, "Wake up, kind giant, the morning is waiting for you." As Pip touched the bough, radiant lavender warmth spread outward, illuminating the silver moss and chasing away the shadows.

One by one, the trees opened their emerald leaves, rejoicing with laughter as the golden sun broke through the canopy. Pip smiled, resting softly upon a glowing leaf as the entire forest came alive.
